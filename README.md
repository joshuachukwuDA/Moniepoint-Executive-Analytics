# Moniepoint Executive Analytics

## Executive Fintech Analytics & Business Intelligence Case Study

A simulated executive analytics project designed to demonstrate how data can be transformed into **business insights, management recommendations, and an executive decision-making story** within a fintech environment.

The project analyzes transaction performance, agent and regional operations, loan portfolio performance, and credit risk using Tableau.

> **Disclaimer:** This is a portfolio simulation created using synthetic banking data. It is not affiliated with Moniepoint and does not represent Moniepoint's actual internal data, performance, systems, or business results.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

The objective of this project was to simulate an executive analytics engagement where management needs a consolidated view of business performance and the key factors affecting growth, operational reliability, and portfolio risk.

Rather than focusing only on dashboard creation, the project follows the full analytical process:

**Data → Analysis → Visualization → Insights → Recommendations → Executive Presentation**

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The analysis focused on five major questions:

1. How is overall transaction performance trending?
2. Which channels and transaction types contribute most to business activity?
3. How do agent and regional performance differ?
4. How healthy is the loan portfolio?
5. What actions should management prioritize based on the analysis?

---

## 🖥️ Executive Analytics Suite

### 1. Business Performance

The dashboard provides an overview of transaction and operational performance.

**Key KPIs:**

* Total Transaction Value: **₦610M**
* Fee Revenue: **₦6M**
* Total Transactions: **500**
* Transaction Success Rate: **80%**
* Average Processing Time: **62.76 seconds**

The dashboard examines monthly transaction trends, fee revenue by channel, transaction type performance, channel success rates, and processing time by state.

### 2. Agent & Regional Performance

This dashboard focuses on agent productivity and regional operational performance.

It examines:

* Agent transaction contribution
* Agent productivity differences
* Regional performance
* State uptime
* Customer experience indicators
* High- and low-performing operational areas

The analysis highlights the concentration of transaction activity among a smaller group of high-performing agents and differences in operational reliability across regions.

### 3. Loan Portfolio & Credit Risk

The loan dashboard evaluates both lending growth and portfolio quality.

**Key KPIs:**

* Total Loans Disbursed: **₦516M**
* Amount Repaid: **₦373M**
* Repayment Rate: **72.3%**
* Outstanding Balance: **₦143M**
* NPL Rate: **10.5%**
* Total Borrowers: **200**

The dashboard analyzes loan distribution by type and state, monthly disbursement trends, repayment performance, interest rates, and NPL status.

### 4. Executive Insights & Recommendations

The insights layer translates the dashboard findings into management-focused conclusions.

The analysis focuses on:

* Transaction reliability
* Agent productivity
* Regional operational performance
* Lending performance
* Portfolio quality
* Credit risk

### 5. Executive Story

A Tableau Story was created to guide an executive audience through the analysis from business performance to regional and agent performance, portfolio risk, insights, and final recommendations.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

### Transaction Performance

Transaction activity is strong, with **₦610M** in transaction value and an overall **80% success rate**.

Web generates the highest fee revenue at approximately **₦1.6M**, while success rates vary across channels, creating an opportunity to investigate failed transactions and improve operational reliability.

### Agent & Regional Performance

Transaction activity is concentrated among a smaller group of high-volume agents.

Regional operational performance also varies, with differences in state uptime highlighting opportunities for targeted operational improvement.

### Loan Portfolio

The loan portfolio reached **₦516M in total disbursements**, with **₦373M repaid** and **₦143M outstanding**.

Education loans represent the largest share of the portfolio at approximately **₦130.6M**.

Monthly lending activity peaked in **October 2023 at approximately ₦53.2M**, before declining toward early 2024.

### Credit Risk

The **10.5% NPL rate** indicates that approximately one in ten loans is classified as non-performing.

While the majority of the portfolio remains healthy, restructured and defaulted loans require continued monitoring and early intervention.

---

## 🎯 Management Recommendations

### 01 — Improve Transaction Reliability

Investigate failed transactions by channel, transaction type, and region to identify the operational drivers behind the 80% success rate.

### 02 — Strengthen Agent Performance

Use high-performing agents as benchmarks and develop targeted support or training strategies for lower-performing agents.

### 03 — Improve Regional Reliability

Investigate regions with lower uptime and identify the operational factors affecting service reliability and customer experience.

### 04 — Scale High-Performing Lending Segments

Continue monitoring high-performing loan products while evaluating whether successful lending patterns can be responsibly expanded.

### 05 — Strengthen Credit Risk Monitoring

Increase early intervention for restructured and defaulted loans while closely monitoring repayment trends and portfolio concentration.

---

The central message from the analysis is:

> **Moniepoint doesn't just have an opportunity to grow. It has an opportunity to grow smarter.**

That means improving transaction reliability, learning from the strongest agents and regions, scaling what works, and maintaining disciplined credit-risk management.
